Case study

Atlas Vines — D2C with 3× the conversion.

A boutique winemaker wanted to sell directly. In 10 weeks we launched a Shopify Plus store that beat every target.

+212%Online revenue, year 1
3.4%Conversion rate
0.6sLoad time
98Mobile PageSpeed

Challenge

Atlas Vines sold exclusively B2B. They wanted to enter the direct-to-consumer channel while keeping a premium feel and complying with the complex regulations around online alcohol sales.

Solution

A bespoke Shopify Plus store with a wine-club subscription system, integration with local payments and shipping, age verification at checkout and editorial content for each wine — premium narrative in editorial format.
