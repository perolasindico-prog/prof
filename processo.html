Process

Four phases. Zero surprises.

We work in short cycles with weekly deliveries. You always see what's being done, why, and how much it costs. This is our methodology, refined over 120+ projects.

Phase 01 · 1 to 2 weeks

Discovery & Strategy

Before designing a single pixel, we want to understand your business.

  • 4-hour immersion workshop
  • Stakeholder interviews
  • Competitor analysis & benchmarks
  • KPI and persona definition
  • Approved information architecture
  • Deliverable: Strategic document (15–30 pages)
Phase 02 · 2 to 4 weeks

Design & Validation

Creative direction, prototyping and testing with real users.

  • Mood boards and art direction
  • Low-fidelity wireframes
  • Design system and component library
  • Clickable Figma prototype
  • Tests with 5 users (moderated)
  • Deliverable: Figma file + brand book
Phase 03 · 3 to 8 weeks

Development

Modular implementation with staging and weekly reviews.

  • Git repo and CI/CD setup
  • Component-by-component build
  • Integration with chosen CMS
  • Staging environment with branch previews
  • Code reviews & automated tests
  • Deliverable: Site on staging + documentation
Phase 04 · 1 week

QA & Launch

Rigorous quality assurance before go-live.

  • Tests on 8 browsers and real devices
  • Accessibility audit (WCAG 2.2 AA)
  • Lighthouse 90+ on every metric
  • Analytics & monitoring setup
  • Team training (1h, recorded)
  • 14 days post-launch warranty included
