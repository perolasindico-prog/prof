About

A small studio with big focus.

We are four people, two time zones and a shared coffee in Lagos. We believe the web can be faster, more beautiful and more human — and we built an entire portfolio proving it.

Our mission

To make professional web design accessible to brands that still believe in doing things well. We work as an extension of your team — not as a distant agency.

How we think

Performance is design. Accessibility is design. SEO is design. Everything we touch is built to perform — not just to look good.

Values

Six principles guide every project.

1. Radical honesty

We say when an idea won't work. And we explain why — with data, not opinions.

2. Performance first

Every kilobyte is a choice. We optimise before we add.

3. Accessibility as a baseline

WCAG 2.2 AA is the minimum. Webs that exclude don't serve.

4. Real documentation

We deliver video tutorials, brand book and clean code. No technical hostages.

5. Sustainable work

Lighter sites use less energy. Green hosting, always.

6. Clients as partners

We pick projects carefully. We do better work when there's trust.
